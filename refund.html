Thank you for choosing Ploxera. We provide digital products such as templates, graphic assets, e-books, and other downloadable content. Because our products are delivered instantly, our refund policy follows EU digital-content regulations and Stripe’s requirements.

1. Digital Product Delivery

All purchases made on Ploxera are digital downloads. Once the payment is completed, customers receive an instant download link by email.

2. Refund Eligibility

Due to the nature of digital products, all sales are generally final. However, we offer refunds under the following conditions:

  • The product was not delivered (you did not receive the download link).
  • A technical issue prevents you from accessing the product and we are unable to provide a working alternative.
  • The product description does not match the delivered item in a significant way.
  • Duplicate purchases were made by mistake.

Cases where refunds are not granted:

  • The customer has already downloaded or accessed the product.
  • The customer changes their mind after purchase.
  • The product did not meet personal or subjective expectations.
  • The purchase was made by mistake but the product was already delivered.

These conditions comply with EU Directive 2011/83/EU regarding digital content and consumer rights.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us within 7 days of your purchase:

Email: support@ploxera.com

Please include:

  • Your order number
  • The email used at purchase
  • A brief explanation of the issue
  • Any relevant screenshots or details

We respond to all refund requests within 48 hours.

4. Unauthorized Payments or Fraud

If you believe someone made a purchase using your payment method without your permission, please contact us immediately. We will work with Stripe and your bank to resolve the issue.

5. Chargebacks

Before initiating a chargeback, please reach out to us. Most issues can be resolved quickly through our support team.
