Refund Policy

Last updated: 2025-12-06

Thank you for choosing Ploxera. We provide digital products including templates, graphic assets, e-books, and other downloadable content. Because our products are delivered instantly, our refund policy complies with EU digital-content regulations and Stripe requirements.

1. Digital Product Delivery

All purchases are delivered as digital downloads. Once payment is completed, customers receive an instant download link by email.

2. Refund Eligibility

Due to the nature of digital products, all sales are generally final. However, refunds may be granted under the following conditions:

  • The product was not delivered (you did not receive the download link).
  • A technical issue prevents access to the product and we cannot provide a working alternative.
  • The product description does not match the delivered item in a significant way.
  • Duplicate purchases were made by mistake.

Refunds are NOT granted when:

  • The customer has already downloaded or accessed the product.
  • The customer changes their mind after purchase.
  • The product did not meet personal or subjective expectations.
  • The purchase was made by mistake but the product was already delivered.

These conditions comply with EU Directive 2011/83/EU regarding digital content.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact us within 7 days of your purchase:

Email: help@ploxera.com

Please include:

  • Your order number
  • The email used during purchase
  • A brief explanation of the issue
  • Any helpful screenshots or details

We respond to all refund requests within 48 hours.

4. Unauthorized Payments or Fraud

If you believe an unauthorized purchase was made using your payment method, contact us immediately. We will work with Stripe and your bank to investigate.

5. Chargebacks

Please contact us before filing a chargeback. Most issues can be resolved quickly through support.

6. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Refund Policy:
